
This project presents an end-to-end analysis of Superstore retail sales data to uncover business insights,
sales trends, customer behavior, and profitability drivers. The dashboard and analysis were built using SQL, Excel,
and data visualization techniques to support business decision-making

   Business Problem

Retail businesses generate massive transactional data daily, but identifying profitable categories, underperforming regions,
and seasonal sales trends is difficult without proper analysis.

Management needed answers for:

-Which product categories generate maximum profit?
-Which states contribute most to revenue?
-What are the monthly sales and profit trends?
-Which sub-categories drive business growth?
-How does profit margin vary across categories?

  Objectives
  
-Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
-Analyze sales and profit trends
-Build KPI-based dashboard
-Identify top-performing categories and states
-Visualize sales distribution geographically
-Generate actionable business insights

Key Insights
-- Technology Category Leads Revenue

Technology contributes nearly 40% of total sales, making it the highest-performing category.

-- Phones are Top-Selling Products

Phones generated the highest revenue among all sub-categories.

--California Dominates Sales

California recorded the highest sales volume among all states.

-- Seasonal Growth Detected

Sales peaked during:

November
December

indicating strong holiday demand trends.

-- Furniture Has Lower Margins

Despite good sales volume, Furniture generates lower profit margins due to higher discounts and operational costs.

Results & Conclusion 

-The analysis successfully identified that while Phones and Chairs drive the highest sales, the company should focus on reducing discounts in the Furniture category to protect margins. Expanding the high-margin Technology segment in underperforming southern states represents the largest growth opportunity.
